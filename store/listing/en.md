**Description** (max 4000)
Get more done with less stress. Pomodoro: Focus Timer helps you work in short, focused sessions followed by refreshing breaks — the simple time-management method that students, developers, writers and professionals around the world swear by.

HOW IT WORKS
• Focus for 25 minutes
• Take a 5-minute short break
• After every 4 focus sessions, enjoy a longer 15-minute break
• Repeat — and watch your focused time add up

DESIGNED TO STAY OUT OF YOUR WAY
• One clean screen: start, pause, reset or skip with a single tap
• A large, easy-to-read countdown with a smooth progress ring
• Calm colors for each mode, so you always know whether to work or rest

FEATURES
• Focus, short break and long break modes
• Adjustable session lengths and long-break frequency
• Optional auto-start for the next session
• Notification when a session ends, even if the app is in the background
• Screen stays awake while the timer runs
• Gentle haptic feedback
• Daily counter of completed focus sessions
• Works on iPhone and iPad
• Available in English and Turkish

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no sign-up, no tracking. Your settings and progress stay on your device.

Whether you're studying for exams, writing code, answering email or tackling your to-do list, Pomodoro: Focus Timer helps you start, stay focused and take breaks before you burn out.
